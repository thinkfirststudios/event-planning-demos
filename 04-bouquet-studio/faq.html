Questions

Are you a wedding planner?

No. I design the room and I run the design on the day. If you want someone to look after guests, seating, hotels and the running order, hire a planner as well. I’ll happily work with one.

What exactly do you do?

Design direction and palette, florals, tablescape and styling, installation and strike, and casting and running the suppliers for the design. There’s a longer list on my services page, along with what I don’t do.

How does your fee work?

It’s a share of what you spend on the room: the florals, the furniture, the linen and the installation. The money page explains it properly.

What is the design consultation?

A paid session where we walk your look through your room and I write up what I’d do. If you go ahead with me, the fee comes off the design fee.

Do you only do weddings?

No. Anniversaries, big birthdays, long lunches, anything with a table worth dressing. Start from a look in the same way.

Do you travel?

I work mostly in the city and the river valley north of it. Further than that is fine, and the travel and extra install time go into the plan so you can see them.

What happens if our date moves?

That’s covered by my contract, and the contract is what counts. Please read that part before you sign and ask me about anything that isn’t clear.

Will you hang flowers from the ceiling?

Overhead installations and candles are agreed with the venue, and the venue’s rules decide. I ask before I design anything for the air. If the answer is no, the design changes.

Is the venue accessible? Can they handle dietary needs?

Those are questions I ask every venue, and I pass on what they tell me. They’re the venue’s answers rather than mine, so please confirm anything that matters with the venue directly.

Who owns the photographs on this site?

The photographers do. Each one is credited under the picture and used under the Pexels licence. I didn’t take them, and none of them shows a client’s event.
