About

A production company, not a party planner

Loadline is a small production team based in Port Calder. We work from a written brief and a signed budget, and the producer you meet on the scope call stays with you to the end.

How we workin practice

One producer. One budget. One running order.

We keep the core team small and book specialist crew per programme. You get a named producer, a budget you sign line by line, and a running order every supplier works from.
