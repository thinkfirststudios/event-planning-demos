Programme

How a programme runs

Eight phases from brief to report. Each one has a named owner and a typical duration, so you know who to call and when.

The phases

Owner and duration

Block widths show relative time for a two-day conference of around 400 delegates. Highlighted phases are the two points where you sign something.

  1. PHASE 01

    Brief and scope call

    Owner: Loadline producerDuration: 1 week

    We read the brief, then a 30-minute call to test dates, numbers and budget.

  2. PHASE 02

    Proposal and budget

    Owner: Producer with your finance leadDuration: 1–2 weeks

    A written scope, an itemised budget and the fee model that fits.

  3. PHASE 03

    Venue and supplier sourcing

    Owner: Loadline producerDuration: 3–6 weeks

    Shortlist, site visits, contracts reviewed with you before signature.

  4. PHASE 04

    Content and speakers

    Owner: Your content lead, with usDuration: 4–8 weeks

    Agenda, speaker briefs, slide deadlines and rehearsal slots.

  1. PHASE 05

    Registration open

    Owner: Loadline delegate teamDuration: 6–10 weeks

    Invitations, the registration link, travel and dietary returns.

  2. PHASE 06

    Technical design and rehearsal

    Owner: Loadline technical leadDuration: 2–4 weeks

    Rig drawings, show files, a full technical rehearsal before doors.

  3. PHASE 07

    Build, show and derig

    Owner: Loadline on-site crewDuration: 1–3 days

    Load-in, the show called from the desk, everything out by the venue deadline.

  4. PHASE 08

    Wrap and report

    Owner: Loadline producerDuration: 2 weeks

    Final invoice against budget, supplier sign-off and a short written report.

The calendar compresses. Our shortest conference lead time is set by venue availability, not by us; ask on the scope call.

Show weekon site

Load-in to derig.

A production office opens the day before. Riggers and AV load in overnight, the technical rehearsal runs in the morning, and doors open to a room that has already done the day once.

Sign-off points

What you approve, and when

  • The proposal and budget (phase 02)
  • Each venue and supplier contract (phase 03)
  • The final running order, five working days out
  • Budget changes over $1,000, in writing, every time
  • The final invoice against the signed budget (phase 08)

Request for proposal

Send us the brief

A page is enough to start. Tell us:

  • Dates, or a window
  • Expected headcount
  • City or region
  • Format: in person, hybrid or virtual
  • Budget band

We reply within two working days to book a 30-minute scope call. A deposit applies once a proposal is signed.
