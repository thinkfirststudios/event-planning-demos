Tell me the room. I’ve probably got an opinion.

What a walkthrough is

We meet in the room in the evening, at the hour your night will actually happen, because a room at lunchtime tells you nothing. It takes about as long as a slow drink. There’s no presentation. You’ll hear where I’d put the bar and what I’d do about the light.

If a room is booked

I walk that room with you, and I ask the room the questions you shouldn’t have to.

If it isn’t

We walk the kinds of room that would work for your night, and I tell you what to ask each of them. I don’t book the room for you, and I don’t take anything from any of them.

What arrives afterwards

One page: what I’d do to the room and what my fee would be. Not a deck.
