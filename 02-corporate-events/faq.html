Questions buyers ask

Answers

Frequently asked

What do you need from us to start?

Dates or a window, expected headcount, a city or region, the format and a budget band. A one-page brief is plenty. Upload it on the brief form or email it to briefs@loadline.example.

How do your fees work?

Three models: a management fee from $9,500 per programme, a fixed project fee from $18,000 for a single-day event up to 250 delegates, or a per-attendee rate from $85 for registration and check-in. Crew day rates start at $410. The proposal tells you which model fits and why.

Do you take commissions from venues?

Our position on commissions and venue rebates is set out in every proposal. [WHAT IS REBATED OR COMMISSIONED, AND DISCLOSED TO WHOM — CONFIRM]

Is there a deposit?

Yes. A deposit applies once a proposal is signed. The deposit, cancellation and force majeure terms are included in every proposal and shown on the brief page.

How far ahead should we book?

A conference of 250 to 800 delegates usually needs 16 to 24 weeks. An offsite can work in 8. Award dinners book early because the rooms do.

Can you work with our procurement process?

Yes. We complete supplier onboarding forms, invoice against purchase orders, and send the in-house and sourced breakdown with the proposal.

Do you handle hybrid and streamed sessions?

Yes. We spec the cameras, the stream and the captioning, and run a separate rehearsal for remote speakers.

Can you name past clients?

Most of our work is under confidentiality agreements. We describe past work by sector and size on the scope call.

Where are you based?

Port Calder. We travel for the work; travel and per-diem costs are itemised in the budget.
