Privacy

What the enquiry form collects

Your name, your email, your date or month, where the room is, roughly how many people, which look you started from, and anything you add in the note. I use them to reply to you and to prepare for a consultation if we book one.

What the contact form collects

Your name, your email, the reason you picked and your message. I use them to reply.

Who sees it

Me. The forms come to my inbox. I don’t share them with other businesses, sell them or add you to a mailing list.

Analytics and cookies

This site has no analytics, no tracking pixels and no cookies of its own. Its typefaces load from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google when a page opens.

Payments

The site takes no payments. If a consultation fee is paid, that happens separately, after you’ve agreed to it.
