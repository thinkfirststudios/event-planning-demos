The three packages

What each includes, and what it doesn't

Full planning

from $9,800

Flat fee, confirmed in your proposal

Starts 12 to 18 months before the wedding

Included

  • A budget and timeline in the first month
  • Venue search, with up to five site visits together
  • A supplier shortlist; we book them and read every contract with you
  • Design direction, floor plan and table layout
  • Monthly meetings and email throughout
  • Rehearsal and the full day, with two planners on site

Not included

  • Venue and supplier fees
  • Rentals, flowers, catering and drink
  • Printed stationery
  • Alcohol licences, permits and marquee approvals (arranged by you and your venue)
  • Travel beyond 90 miles
  • Team accommodation for multi-day weddings

Scope

What no package includes

Our fee covers our time and our team. Everything we book on your behalf is paid by you, directly to the supplier.

  • Venue and supplier fees
  • Rentals, flowers, catering and drink
  • Printed stationery
  • Alcohol licences, permits and marquee approvals (arranged by you and your venue)
  • Travel beyond 90 miles
  • Team accommodation for multi-day weddings

When we quote separately

Weddings over 180 guests, weekends with more than one event, and anything more than 90 miles from the studio.
