Planning & coordination

Three ways to work with us

Some couples hand us the whole year. Some have a venue and half a plan. Some only want someone to run the day. Each is a different job with a different start date, so we price and staff them separately.

The table

Tablescapes & rooms

Most of a wedding is spent at a table, so that's where our design starts: the plates, the linen, the flowers and where the candles sit.

We draw the floor plan before anything is ordered and check every table against the venue's measurements. On the day we set the room ourselves, an hour before the doors open.

Packages

Three different jobs

Prices are where each package starts. Your proposal confirms the fee once we know your date, numbers and venue. Weddings over 180 guests, multi-day weekends and anything beyond 90 miles are quoted separately.

Full planning

from $9,800

Flat fee, confirmed in your proposal

Starts 12 to 18 months before the wedding

Included

  • A budget and timeline in the first month
  • Venue search, with up to five site visits together
  • A supplier shortlist; we book them and read every contract with you
  • Design direction, floor plan and table layout
  • Monthly meetings and email throughout
  • Rehearsal and the full day, with two planners on site

Not included

  • Venue and supplier fees
  • Rentals, flowers, catering and drink
  • Printed stationery
  • Alcohol licences, permits and marquee approvals (arranged by you and your venue)
  • Travel beyond 90 miles
  • Team accommodation for multi-day weddings
