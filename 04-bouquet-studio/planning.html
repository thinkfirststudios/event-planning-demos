How it goesfrom the first call to the morning after, in order

This is everything that happens, in the order it happens. How long it all takes depends on your event, so I’ll tell you that when we talk.

Before you’ve hired me

The first call

You tell me what it is. I tell you whether I’m free and whether I’m the right person. Sometimes I’m not.

The consultation

We go through the event, the money and what’s worrying you. I write down what it would take and what the fee would be.

The contract

You read it, you ask me about anything in it, and a deposit secures your date.

If I think your budget won’t do it, I’ll say so in the first conversation.

The big decisions

The budget

We build it line by line, before anybody falls in love with anything.

The venue

I go and see the options, ask what they don’t put in the brochure, and take you to the ones worth your time.

The suppliers

Caterer first, then everyone else. I get the quotes, read the small print and tell you what I’d do. You sign.

The long middle

The guests

Invitations out, replies in, the people who never reply chased. Dietary and access questions go to the venue and the caterer.

The look

Flowers, tables and the room, agreed with you and handed to the people who’ll build them.

The running order

One document that says who does what and when. Every supplier gets it, and so do you.

The week before

I ring everyone. Every delivery, every arrival, every last number gets checked again.

This is the boring part. It’s also why the day works.

The day

The morning

I’m there before the first delivery. The room goes up, the suppliers arrive, and I walk it all before anyone sees it.

The event

You go to your party. I stay on the floor with the running order and deal with whatever comes up.

The end of the night

Last orders, taxis, lost property, and everything out of the room when the venue says so.

If you see me panicking, something’s badly wrong. You won’t.

The morning after

The clear-up

Hire goes back, flowers get passed on, and anything left at the venue gets collected.

The loose ends

I check the final bills against what was agreed, and I send you what you need to pay and what you left behind.

Then I send you a message, and then I go back to bed.

If that sounds like the kind of help you want, start by telling me what kind of event it is.
