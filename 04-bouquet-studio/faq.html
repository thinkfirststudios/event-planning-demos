Questions

What exactly does an event planner do?

I take your event from a rough idea to the last van leaving: the budget, the timeline, the venue search, the suppliers, the guest logistics and the day itself. There’s a longer list on my services page, along with what I don’t do.

Can I hire you just for the day?

No. I only work from the start. I can’t run a day I didn’t plan, because everything that makes it work was decided months earlier.

Do you only do weddings?

No. Parties, long dinners, lunches in gardens, anything with a guest list and a running order. Pick the closest kind of event and we’ll start from there.

How does your fee work?

It’s one planning fee, worked out from how much I’m taking on and how long we’ll be planning for. The money page explains it properly.

What is the planning consultation?

A paid session where we go through the event and the money, and I write up what it would take. If you go ahead with me, the fee comes off the planning fee.

Do you take commission from suppliers?

No. You pay your suppliers directly, in your name, and they pay me nothing.

Will I have to deal with the suppliers myself?

You’ll meet the ones you want to meet and you’ll sign their contracts. The chasing and the day-to-day come to me.

Do you travel?

I work mostly in the city and the river valley north of it. Further than that is fine, and the travel and extra days go into the fee so you can see them.

What happens if our date moves?

That’s covered by my contract, and the contract is what counts. Please read that part before you sign and ask me about anything that isn’t clear.

Can we hang things from the ceiling or have candles?

Overhead installations and candles are agreed with the venue, and the venue’s rules decide. I ask before anything gets planned. If the answer is no, the plan changes.

Who sorts out alcohol, permits and marquees?

Alcohol, permits and temporary structures are your responsibility and the venue’s. I’ll raise the questions and chase the answers, but I don’t hold them.

Is the venue accessible? Can they handle dietary needs?

Those are questions I ask every venue, and I pass on what they tell me. They’re the venue’s answers rather than mine, so please confirm anything that matters with the venue directly.

Who owns the photographs on this site?

The photographers do. Each one is credited under the picture and used under the Pexels licence. I didn’t take them, and none of them shows a client’s event.
