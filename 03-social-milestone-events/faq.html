What people ask before they book

Planning a

How far ahead should I book?

Six to twelve weeks is typical. We can sometimes take a party on three weeks' notice if the venue is already booked and the guest count is under 60. Ask and we'll tell you straight away.

Is the estimate on the add-ons page a quote?

No. It's a rough total built from our current rates so you can see the shape of the cost. We send a written quote after a call, and that is the only figure we hold to.

Is there a deposit?

Yes. A deposit of 30% of the quoted package holds your date. The full deposit, cancellation and postponement terms are in the agreement we send with your quote.

Do you find the venue?

On The Full Flag we shortlist venues and visit two with you. On the other packages you book the venue and we work with it. We don't take commission from venues.

Who handles the bar and any permits?

Alcohol service, event permits and tents or other temporary structures are the responsibility of the host and the venue, not Pennant. We'll put your bar supplier and venue in touch and add their timings to the run sheet.

Who looks after children at the party?

Supervision of children is arranged by the host and the venue. Our team sets up, styles the room and runs the timings, and we leave the guest list and supervision to you.

Why are there no photos of the children's and teen parties?

We never put photographs of children on our site. For those occasions we show the rooms and tables we dress, and we're glad to talk through a plan for yours.

Can you plan around religious or family customs?

Yes. Tell us the order of the day and who leads each part, and we plan the room and timings around it. Your family or your officiant has the final word.

Can you keep it a surprise?

Yes. Every email and booking goes through you, and we'll use whatever name and number you give us.

Can you tell me if a venue is accessible, or cater to dietary needs?

We don't make promises on behalf of venues or caterers. Tell us what your guests need and we'll put the question to them in writing and pass on their answer.

What happens if a rental is damaged?

Rental damage and loss are covered by the rental terms in your agreement. Delivery and collection are $120 within 25 miles, and we collect the next morning by 11am unless the venue needs us out the same night.

What's the smallest party you'll take on?

Ten guests, with a minimum spend of $650. Below that, our add-ons can be hired on their own with delivery.

Where do you work?

Larkfield and the Wren Valley, up to about 40 miles from our Kiln Lane studio. Beyond 25 miles, delivery is quoted per trip.
