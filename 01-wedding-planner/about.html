About the studio

Four people · one wedding a weekend

The studio

Why we stay small

We started Quillon & Crane because the hardest part of a wedding isn't the design; it's the hundred small decisions in between, and who keeps track of them.

So we cap the season at 22 weddings and never take two on one weekend. Every couple has one lead planner from the first meeting, and two of us are on site on the day.

We work from a shared studio at 41 Larch Yard in Wrenmoor, and travel across the Ashby Valley and anywhere within 90 miles.
