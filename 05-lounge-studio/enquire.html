What a walkthrough is

We meet in the room at the hour your party will actually happen, because a room at the wrong time tells you nothing. It takes about as long as a slow coffee. There’s no presentation. You’ll hear where everything goes and what I’d do with the colour.

If a room is booked

I walk that room with you, and I ask the room the questions you shouldn’t have to.

If it isn’t

We walk the kinds of room that would work for your party, and I tell you what to ask each of them. I don’t book the room for you, and I don’t take anything from any of them.

What arrives afterwards

One page: what I’d do to the room and what my fee would be. Not a deck.
