What's the difference between full, partial and month-of?

Full planning starts 12 to 18 months out and covers everything from the venue search onwards. Partial planning starts once you have a venue, usually 6 to 9 months out, and fills in the rest. Month-of coordination starts 8 weeks before: you've planned it, and we take over the final weeks and run the day.

Compare them side by side →

When does month-of actually start?

Eight weeks before the wedding, with a two-hour handover meeting. If a month is still open, we can take month-of coordination up to four weeks before.

What isn't included?

Venue and supplier fees; Rentals, flowers, catering and drink; Printed stationery; Alcohol licences, permits and marquee approvals (arranged by you and your venue); Travel beyond 90 miles; Team accommodation for multi-day weddings. Our fee covers our time and our team; anything we book for you is paid directly to the supplier.

How does pricing work?

Each package has a flat fee, starting at $9,800 for full planning, $5,400 for partial and $2,450 for month-of. Your proposal confirms the fee. Weddings over 180 guests, multi-day weekends and anything beyond 90 miles are quoted separately.

Travel within 90 miles of the studio is included. Beyond that, we quote travel and accommodation in your proposal.
