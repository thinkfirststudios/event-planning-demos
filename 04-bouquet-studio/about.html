Hello, it’s meDelphine Ivey, and the people who hold my ladder

How I started

I started out sweeping the floor of a flower shop and arguing with the owner about colour. She let me do a table once to shut me up. People stood around it all night, and that was me finished for any other kind of job.

What I’m good at

Rooms. Walking into a dull function suite and seeing what it could be. Colour that looks like a decision. Getting a lot of flowers to look as if nobody touched them.

What I find hard

Saying no to people I like. Early mornings. Letting a flower die in a bucket because it isn’t right, when it cost me a lot and I grew it myself.

What I won’t compromise on

I won’t copy a look from somebody else’s wedding. I won’t use flowers that are wrong for the month and pretend they’re fine. And I won’t tell you something works when it doesn’t.

My mother still asks when I’m getting a proper job.
