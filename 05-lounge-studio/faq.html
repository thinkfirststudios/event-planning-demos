FAQ

The ones I get every time

Do you serve the drinks?

No. The room holds the licence, the room’s staff pour, and the studio does not serve alcohol. I don’t buy it, stock it or supply it.

Who holds the licence?

The room, under its own licence. I work with its team on where the bar stands and what it offers. Everything about who is served, and how much, is the room’s responsibility under that licence.

What time does it have to end, and who decides?

The room decides, under its own rules and permits and the local noise rules. I ask at the walkthrough and tell you what they say. I can’t get you a later finish, and I don’t promise one.

How many people can a room hold?

That’s the room’s number and the fire marshal’s, and I won’t guess at it here. I get it from the room at the start and plan from it.

Do you book the room?

No. You take the room and the contract is between you and them. If you haven’t got one yet, we can walk the kinds of room that would work and I’ll tell you what to ask.

What happens if the date moves?

That’s covered in my contract, and it’s better read there than paraphrased here. You’ll see it before anything is signed.

Do you do weddings?

Not the ceremony. That’s a different job, and there are people who do it far better than I would. The party afterwards, and the family celebrations around it, I’d love.

Can I have a lot of colour?

Please. Tell me the colour at the walkthrough and I’ll tell you how far we can take it in that room.

What do you ask the room about access and dietary needs?

I ask how someone using a wheelchair gets in and gets to the bathroom, what the kitchen can and can’t do about allergies, and where a guest can go if they need somewhere quiet. I pass on what the room tells me. It’s the room’s answer, not my promise.

Who owns the photographs on this site?

The photographers do. Each one is named on their print, and they’re used under the Pexels licence. They show the kind of idea I’m describing. None of them is a Late Room event and I didn’t take any of them.

What happens after the walkthrough?

A single page arrives with what I’d do and what my fee would be. If you’d like to go ahead, a deposit holds the date, and its terms are in my contract.
