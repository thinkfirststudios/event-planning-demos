What the forms collect

The quick message form collects your name, email, the occasion, a date and a message. The quote form adds an optional phone number, a guest count, an optional venue, a budget band, an optional milestone and your estimate selections. Neither form asks for anything about a child.

How we use it

Only to reply to you and prepare a quote. We don't sell or share it, and we don't add you to a mailing list unless you tick the optional box on the quote form.

The estimator

The estimator runs in your browser. Your selections are kept in the page address so you can share or bookmark them. Nothing is stored on our side until you send a form.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics or advertising pixels. It loads fonts from Google Fonts.
