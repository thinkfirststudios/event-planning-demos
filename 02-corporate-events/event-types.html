What we produce

Conferences and summits

Lead time16–24 weeksTypical size120–1,500FormatIn person or hybrid

A plenary room set theatre-style, three to eight breakouts, catering in the gaps and registration from the first invitation. Most of our conference work is one or two days.

A small room and a long agenda

Leadership offsites

Lead time8–12 weeksTypical size12–60FormatIn person

Travel, rooming, a working room with daylight, facilitators if you need them, and dinners that do not run late. Quiet logistics for a senior group.

One stage moment, done properly

Product launches

Lead time10–16 weeksTypical size80–600FormatIn person, streamed

A reveal built around a single running order: stage, screens, press seating, partner sessions afterwards, and a stream for everyone else.

Seated dinner, envelopes, fast turnovers

Award dinners

Lead time12–20 weeksTypical size150–900FormatIn person

Table plans, a presenter script, walk-up music cleared by the venue, and a stage manager who keeps the categories moving between courses.

Everyone in the company, one message

Town halls and internal comms

Lead time4–8 weeksTypical size100–3,000FormatHybrid, multi-site

One main room, satellite sites on a live link, a moderated question queue and captions on the stream.

Stand, freight and the meeting diary

Trade-show presence

Lead time12+ weeksTypical sizeStand team of 4–30FormatExhibition

Stand design brief, builder sourcing, freight, the staffing rota and the meeting-room calendar. We work to the organiser's exhibitor manual.

Request for proposal

Send us the brief

A page is enough to start. Tell us:

  • Dates, or a window
  • Expected headcount
  • City or region
  • Format: in person, hybrid or virtual
  • Budget band

We reply within two working days to book a 30-minute scope call. A deposit applies once a proposal is signed.
