Stage, sound and screen

Technical production

Our technical lead designs the stage, sound, lighting and screens around your running order. We draw the rig, write the show files, book the crew and sit at the desk on the day.

Every show gets a full technical rehearsal before doors, with your speakers on the stage they will use.

How a programme runs

Eight phases, one producer

Each phase has a named owner and a typical duration. The block widths show relative time for a mid-size conference.

  1. PHASE 01

    Brief and scope call

    Owner: Loadline producerDuration: 1 week
  2. PHASE 02

    Proposal and budget

    Owner: Producer with your finance leadDuration: 1–2 weeks
  3. PHASE 03

    Venue and supplier sourcing

    Owner: Loadline producerDuration: 3–6 weeks
  4. PHASE 04

    Content and speakers

    Owner: Your content lead, with usDuration: 4–8 weeks
  1. PHASE 05

    Registration open

    Owner: Loadline delegate teamDuration: 6–10 weeks
  2. PHASE 06

    Technical design and rehearsal

    Owner: Loadline technical leadDuration: 2–4 weeks
  3. PHASE 07

    Build, show and derig

    Owner: Loadline on-site crewDuration: 1–3 days
  4. PHASE 08

    Wrap and report

    Owner: Loadline producerDuration: 2 weeks
