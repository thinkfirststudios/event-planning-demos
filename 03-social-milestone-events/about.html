About Pennant

A small team with a van and a warehouse of chairs

How we started

Pennant began with a stack of folding chairs and a borrowed van for a family seventieth. People kept asking where the chairs came from, and then who had planned the night.

Today we're three people, a studio on Kiln Lane and our own rental stock: chairs, tables, linen, tableware, backdrops and lights. Owning the stock is why we can show you a rate for each item rather than a vague "décor package".

We work across Larkfield and the Wren Valley, in halls, restaurants, gardens and hired marquees.
