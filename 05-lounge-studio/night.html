The shape of a nightin the order it happens

The door, the turn, the last hour, the strike. There are no times on this page, because the room sets those. This is what actually happens, and who’s doing what while it does.

The door

nobody goes first

What I’m doing
Standing where I can see the entrance and the bar at once. I’m watching for the first person who doesn’t know anyone, and I’ve already given them somewhere to go.
What the room’s doing
The room’s staff are set and the bar’s open. Its door team, if it has one, is doing what the room’s rules say.
What you shouldn’t worry about
Being on time. Be ready to enjoy your own party before anyone else arrives, and leave the room to me.

If they can see the bar from the door, they’ve already relaxed.

The turn

when it tips

What I’m doing
Calling it. When the room’s ready I bring the lamps down, cue the food and move the music on, all within a few breaths of each other.
What the room’s doing
Landing the food and keeping the bar moving, on the cue we agreed at the walkthrough.
What you shouldn’t worry about
Speeches. If you’re making one, I’ll tell you when, and I’ll make sure people are holding something.

Nobody notices the turn. That’s how you know it went right.

The last hour

is the whole job

What I’m doing
Keeping the room together. Lights stay down, the coats stay easy, and the music gets better rather than louder.
What the room’s doing
Deciding how it ends. The finish, the sound level and any last orders are the room’s rules, agreed before the night, and I work inside them.
What you shouldn’t worry about
Who’s still there. The people who stay are the ones you’ll talk about it with.

Anyone can start a party. The end is where I earn the fee.

The strike

then it goes back

What I’m doing
Walking the room with its manager once the crew has taken everything down: every lamp, every cable, every chair back where the room had it.
What the room’s doing
Closing up. By the end it should look as though we were never there, and my crew is the reason it does.
What you shouldn’t worry about
Anything. You went home. That was the idea.

You leave with your shoes in your hand. I leave last.
