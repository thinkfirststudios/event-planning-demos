Privacy

The enquiry form

When you book a walkthrough, this site collects your name and email, the date or month of your party, what the party is for, whether a room is booked, a rough idea of how many people, and anything else you choose to write. It goes to Idris Vane so he can reply about your party. It isn’t sold, shared with other businesses or added to a mailing list.

The contact form

This collects your name, your email, the reason you’re writing and your message, and is used only to reply.

Booking the walkthrough

Once a time is agreed by email, a calendar invitation is sent. The calendar service used to send it receives your name, your email address and the time. It does not receive anything else you told us.

Analytics and cookies

This site has no analytics, no advertising tags and no chat tool, and it sets no cookies.

Fonts

The typefaces load from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your browser’s request for them, including your IP address.

Asking about your information

Write to idris@thelateroom.example to ask what is held about you, or to have it removed.
