How it works

How a party comes together

Four steps, usually over six to twelve weeks.

  1. Step 1

    A call

    Twenty minutes on the phone or at the studio. We ask about the date, the guest count, the venue if you have one, and the person it's for. You get a written quote within two working days.

  2. Step 2

    The plan

    Once the deposit is paid, we draft a styling plan and a run sheet. On The Bunting and The Full Flag you see a sample table before anything is ordered.

  3. Step 3

    The bookings

    We confirm rentals from our stock and, on the larger packages, shortlist and book suppliers for you. You sign their contracts directly, so you always know who you're paying.

  4. Step 4

    The day

    Our crew arrives with the van, sets the room and walks it with you or the venue. The coordinator keeps the run sheet, then we pack down and collect.

Start with a number

Use the estimator to see a rough total, then send it to us as a quote request. A deposit of 30% holds the date once you've seen the written quote.
