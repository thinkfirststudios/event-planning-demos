Hello, it’s meDelphine Ivey, and the people who carry the other end of the table

How I started

I started out carrying plates in a hotel function suite, then did a spell in a flower shop, and spent both jobs watching what went wrong at other people’s parties. Then I started telling people what was about to go wrong. Somebody suggested I get paid for it.

What I’m good at

Budgets that hold up. Asking a venue the question they hoped you wouldn’t. Reading a room at the point it’s about to dip, and getting the next thing moving before anyone notices.

What I find hard

Saying no to people I like. Telling someone their budget won’t do what they want it to. Early mornings, which is unfortunate in this job.

What I won’t compromise on

I won’t take money from a supplier for sending you their way. I won’t tell you something’s fine when it isn’t. And I won’t take on more events than I can be at myself.

My mother still asks when I’m getting a proper job.
