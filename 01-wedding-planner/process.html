The process

From first message to the last table cleared

Most of what you pay a planner for is the process. Here it is, step by step, with who does each part and when.

Check a dateBook a consultation →
Six steps

What happens, and when

01

Check a date and enquire

Choose a month in the availability grid and send the form with your date, rough guest count and budget range. Those three let us reply with something useful.

  • WhoImogen
  • WhenWithin two working days
02

The consultation

Forty-five minutes, free, by video or at the studio in Wrenmoor. We talk through what you've booked, what worries you and which package fits.

  • WhoImogen or Theo
  • WhenUsually within a week of your enquiry
03

Proposal and booking

A written proposal setting out the scope, the fee, the team and the deposit that holds your date. Nothing is booked until you sign.

  • WhoTheo
  • WhenWithin a week of the consultation
04

Planning

Budget, suppliers, design and floor plan, at the pace your package sets. Every supplier contract is read with you before you sign it.

  • WhoYour lead planner
  • WhenFrom 18 months to 8 weeks before
05

Final weeks

We confirm every supplier and timing, issue the run sheet and walk the venue with you.

  • WhoYour planner and Jules
  • WhenThe last 8 weeks
06

The day, and after

We set the room, run the timeline and stay until the room is cleared. The following week we return anything hired on your behalf and close the supplier list with you.

  • WhoThe day team named in your proposal
  • WhenThe wedding day and the week after
