Capabilities

What we do, and who does it

Eight capabilities, each with what our own team does and what we source from partners. Procurement asks; we answer it here.

Rooms that fit the agenda

Venue sourcing

In-houseShortlist, site visits, negotiationSourcedThe venue itselfLead time3–6 weeks

We shortlist three to five venues against the brief: capacity by layout, breakout count, load-in access, power and the rate card. We visit, we negotiate, and you see each contract before you sign it.

Stage, sound, lighting and screens

AV and technical production

In-houseDesign, show calling, stage managementSourcedKit hire, riggers, camera crewLead time2–4 weeks

Our technical lead designs the show and calls it from the desk. Kit and crew come from production partners we have worked with before, booked and briefed by us.

One link, one inbox, one list

Registration and delegate management

In-houseSet-up, comms, check-in staffSourcedThe registration platformLead time6–10 weeks

We set up the registration site on a platform you approve, write the invitations, handle travel and rooming, and staff the check-in desk. Access and dietary needs are collected on the platform, not on this website.

Breaks that run to the agenda

Catering management

In-houseMenus, counts, service planSourcedKitchen and service staffLead time4 weeks

We brief the venue kitchen or an outside caterer against the running order: break lengths, dinner service, dietary counts from registration, and a service plan the caterer signs.

Every speaker knows where to stand

Speaker and content management

In-houseBriefs, deadlines, rehearsalsSourcedModerators and hosts, if neededLead time4–8 weeks

Speaker briefs, slide deadlines, rehearsal slots and a green room with a running order on the wall. We chase the decks so your team does not have to.

A running order to the minute

Run-of-show and on-site delivery

In-houseRunning order, on-site teamSourcedSecurity and stewardsLead timeFinal 2 weeks

A written running order shared with every supplier, a production office on site, and a producer with the authority to make the call.

Wayfinding people actually follow

Branding and signage

In-houseSign schedule, floor plan, installSourcedPrint and fabricationLead time3 weeks

Directional signs, stage graphics and the lectern panel, laid out on the floor plan before anything is printed. Your design team supplies the artwork; we handle sizes, print and install.

A short report, two weeks after

Post-event reporting

In-houseReport and reconciliationSourcedNoneLead time2 weeks after the event

Final costs against the signed budget, supplier sign-off, attendance from the registration platform and a list of what we would change next time.

Access and dietary needs

Tell us access and dietary requirements in the brief. We add them to the venue shortlist questions, ask each venue in writing, and share the answers with you before you choose. Delegate-level needs are collected on the registration platform, never on this site.

Request for proposal

Send us the brief

A page is enough to start. Tell us:

  • Dates, or a window
  • Expected headcount
  • City or region
  • Format: in person, hybrid or virtual
  • Budget band

We reply within two working days to book a 30-minute scope call. A deposit applies once a proposal is signed.
