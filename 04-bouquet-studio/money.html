How the money works

What the planning fee is

I charge a planning fee for planning your event and running it on the day. It’s one fee, written down, for that event. There are no levels to choose between, because I only work one way: from the start, on all of it.

How it’s worked out

It depends on how much I’m taking on and how long we’ll be planning for. A wedding spread over a weekend with guests coming from everywhere takes far more of my time than a dinner in one room, and the fee says so.

I don’t charge by the head and I don’t take a cut of what you spend. I also take no commission from suppliers, so I’ve no reason to steer you towards spending more.

What the consultation is

Before anything else we have a paid planning consultation. It takes me a good part of a day: I read what you’ve sent, we go through the event together, and I write up what it would take, what I’d do first and what I think the fee would be. You keep those notes.

I charge for it because it’s real work, and because it’s how I make sure the people I sit down with are serious. I’ll tell you the consultation fee in my first reply, before you’ve agreed to anything.

When it’s credited

If you go ahead with me, the consultation fee comes off the planning fee. If you don’t go ahead, what you paid for was the consultation and the notes.

What the deposit does

If we go ahead, a deposit secures your date. What it is and how it works are set out in my contract. I don’t summarise that part here, because the contract is the thing that counts.

What isn’t included

  • The event itself: the venue, the food, the flowers, the music and everything else you pay your suppliers for. Those bills come to you, in your name.
  • Anything on my list of things I don’t do.

What makes it cost more

  • A short run-up, because the same work has to happen faster.
  • More than one event, such as a weekend of them.
  • More than one site, or a site with nothing on it, like a field or a garden.
  • Distance. Travel and extra days on site go into the fee, and you’ll see them written down.
