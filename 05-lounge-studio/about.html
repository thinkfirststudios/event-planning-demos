Hello

I’m Idris

I’ll be the one by the door

How I started

On the floor of other people’s parties, carrying things. You learn a lot about a room when you’re the person moving the chairs. I learned why a room empties long before I learned anything about flowers.

What I’m good at

Reading a room that’s empty and telling you what it’ll be like full. Where everything goes. How much colour is too much, which is more than you think. I can stand at the back of a party and tell you what’s about to go wrong before it does.

What I find hard

Leaving things alone. I’d rearrange every restaurant I eat in. My crew will confirm this and they’re tired of it.

What I won’t compromise on

The strike, the crew being looked after, and being in the room myself. If I’ve said yes to your party, you get me, not someone I’ve sent.

What most parties get wrong

They play it safe. Beige is what happens when everyone’s afraid of being wrong. And they spend everything on the first impression and nothing on the end. What people remember is how it felt when it was late and the room was still good.

Be brave with the colour. Then spend the rest on the last hour.
