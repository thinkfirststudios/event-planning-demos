I’m Idrisby the door

How I started

On the floor of other people’s parties, carrying things. You learn a lot about a room when you’re the person moving the chairs. I learned what time a room empties and why long before I learned anything about lamps.

What I’m good at

Reading a room that’s empty and telling you what it’ll be like full. Where the bar goes. When to turn the lights down and how far. I can stand at the back of a party and tell you what’s about to go wrong before it does.

What I find hard

Leaving things alone. I’d move every lamp in every restaurant I eat in. My crew will confirm this and they’re tired of it.

What I won’t compromise on

The strike, the crew being looked after, and being in the room myself. If I’ve said yes to your night, you get me, not someone I’ve sent.

What most parties get wrong

They spend everything on the first impression and nothing on the end. People forget the flowers by the time they’ve found their coat. What they remember is how it felt when it was late and the room was still good.

Spend it on the light. Then spend the rest on the last hour.
